Afrofusion cateringSan Antonio

The Drop by Mulberry had great food and a website that made you work to find it.

what I noticed

Too many colours. Buttons that all said different things. Copy written to impress the industry. The person booking an event just wanted to stop worrying about food.

what I built

Mapped the customer journey first. Gave every section one job. Stripped the design back so the food did the work. Rewrote every line for the person booking.

what changed

Visitors moved through the site the way it was built for them. Engagement lifted from day one.

Simple is clarity.
